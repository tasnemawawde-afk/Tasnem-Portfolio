# Product

Tasnem's personal engineering portfolio — a professional website showcasing her background in Electrical & Computer Engineering, technical skills, engineering and AI projects, experience, and training.

## Purpose

The portfolio serves as a professional complement to Tasnem's CV and can be shared with potential employers and recruiters when applying for engineering roles.

## Target Audience

- Recruiters and hiring managers
- Engineering teams and technical interviewers
- Potential professional collaborators

## Main Sections

- Hero / Introduction
- About Me
- Education
- Technical Skills
- Projects
- Experience
- Training & Certificates
- CV
- Contact

## Goals

- Present Tasnem's engineering background in a clean and professional format
- Highlight projects in VLSI, digital systems, MEMS, and AI
- Clearly present relevant technical skills, tools, training, and experience
- Allow visitors to view or download Tasnem's CV
- Provide links to GitHub and LinkedIn and an easy way to get in contact
- Create a responsive and accessible portfolio suitable for desktop and mobile
